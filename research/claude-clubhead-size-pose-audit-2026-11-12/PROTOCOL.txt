1. HYPOTHESES (more than one may hold)
  G  generic dimensions or shape wrong for this club (drawing-to-mm, aspect, heel/toe/hosel proportions)
  D  camera / depth / scale wrong
  O  orientation or reference transform wrong (loft, face, roll; sign or frame)
  E  observed edge identity / objective coverage wrong (what the fit calls the sole; hidden bottom)
  R  face-plane drawing versus the rear body the camera sees

2. MEASUREMENTS
  M0 Pins, dirty states, input hashes before and after. One unmodified replay per shot at the current pin.
  M1 Ledger: the generic outline by anatomical part in mm, with its lineage (kiosk drawing -> JSON), units,
     x/y scale, face-centre datum, hosel foot. Checked by recomputing from the JSON and the drawing source.
  M2 Is the supplied orientation applied? Project the outline with the supplied angles and with loft, face and
     roll each set to zero; report the change of the projected outline in px. (Tests the owner's statement that
     the model is not tilted at all.)
  M3 Rigid-descent check, model-free. Per frame: the ferrule (dark gap on the shaft axis), the top of the head
     at the toe, the lowest visible row of the head. In mm at the supplied depth and in ball diameters
     (depth-free ruler). RULE: between two consecutive frames, if ferrule-to-toe-top changes by less than 4 mm
     while ferrule-to-visible-bottom shrinks by more than 8 mm, the bottom is OCCLUDED on the later frame (E);
     a size or scale error cannot do that, and a loft change would shrink both.
  M4 Silhouette registration. Observed: |frame - pre-swing median| over max(4 sigma, floor), closed 5x7, largest
     component, column-filled right of the hosel (floor 8 DN; 5 and 12 for sensitivity). Model: the application's
     outline plus sole strip at the supplied orientation and depth. Cost: XOR area inside the window right of the
     hosel. For each variant the two image translations are re-optimised (0.25 px grid, +-30 px).
       frames  IN-AIR     the frame at least 4 ms before the application's contact (shot 1 f42, shot 3 f42, shot 4 f43)
               NEAR       the application's last-rest frame (f43, f43, f44)
       variants, one at a time about the supplied values:
               size 0.85 ... 1.15 step 0.05; face +-10, +20, +30; loft -10, +10, +20; roll -6, -3, +3, +6
       windows FULL (whole head) and VISIBLE (model clipped at the observed lowest row)
     RULES (fixed now):
       G/D  "size wrong" if on the IN-AIR frames, FULL window, the best size is 0.90 or less, or 1.10 or more, on at
            least 2 of 3 shots AND improves XOR by 15 % or more over size 1.00. G and D are not separable by size
            alone; the ball diameter in px against 42.67 mm bounds D at the ball.
       O    an angle variant is "supported" if it improves XOR by 15 % or more in the same direction on 2 of 3 shots.
       E    "placement, not size" if on the NEAR frames translation alone (VISIBLE window) cuts XOR by half or more
            against the application's pose AND the registered model's bottom is then more than 5 mm below the
            lowest visible row.
       R    after the best single variant, XOR above 15 % of the observed area means a shape/representation
            residual remains; say where it is.
  M5 Shaft: observed ridge line against the model's shaft axis after M4's registration, px and mm, with the
     in-plane hosel-foot construction examined in 3-D (deduction, then compared).
  M6 Proposed overlay for every placed shot at native scale beside the raw frame and the application's: full
     outline, clipped at the visible line, hidden part dashed. Overhang and uncovered areas reported for both.

  Question. Are the D-plane step's inputs (attack angle, launch angle) wrong on shots 3 and 4, and does correcting
  them move the impact reading toward the photographed marks?
  Two possible flaws in MY OWN earlier numbers (results/DPLANE-CHECK.txt), found by reading before running:
    a. ball_track_lib used the radar's ball speed as the ball's FULL speed. The OPS reads the speed along its line of
       sight. With the image motion fixed, a too-small full speed gives a too-high launch angle.

       last frame. The head's direction turns on its arc, so that is not the direction at contact. The application
       reads a quadratic tangent at its last tracked frame.

  the resting ball then sits 89 / 104 / 97 mm above the surface instead of 21 mm; its launch and delivery code
  instead infer the pitch from the resting ball. Angles "against the camera axis" and angles "against the horizon"
  therefore differ by about 2 degrees, the same on every shot. Both are reported.
  T1 Ball launch. Refit the ball track with the radar reading as the component of the ball's velocity along the line
     from the OPS (camera frame -85, +47, -20 mm) to the ball: (i) at the tee, (ii) 6 ms after contact (sensitivity).
     Old behaviour stays the default of ball_track_lib so earlier results reproduce. Report launch against the camera
     axis and against the horizon (pitch inferred the application's way: lens 95 mm, ball centre 21.35 mm above the
     same surface; also with the ball 25.4 mm higher, the owner's mat, since it is not known what the unit stands on).
     RULE: if the refit launch against the horizon is within 2 deg of the application's camera-fallback launch on
     shots 3 and 4, my statement that the fallback "reads 3-4 deg low" is withdrawn.
  T2 Attack. On every frame before contact where it is found (up to 5): the ferrule (clear of the turf), the lowest
     visible row of the head on the shaft axis, and the application's own tracked point (_clubhead_anchor run
     read-only on the same pixels). Each to mm at the application's depth for that frame. Per point: chord of each
     interval, and the quadratic tangent at the last frame and at mid-contact (ball-track instant).
     RULE: "the application's attack angle is spoiled by the turf line" is supported if, on shots 3 and 4, its tracked

ADDENDUM 11 (13 Nov 2026; written before running). THE HOSEL LENGTH FROM EACH SWING ALONE
  Owner's objections: (a) hosel and ferrule lengths differ between clubs, so a fixed length will not transfer;
  (b) remembering a length per club number fails when the player changes model under the same number. So the
  length must come from the swing being read, with no memory. Addendum 8's attempt (one clear-air frame, solved
  together with the turn) gave 51.5 / 67.4 / 60.3 mm for one club and was discarded.
  Idea tested here: the length lies along the shaft axis, and points on that axis do not move when the club turns
  about it. Measure it along the axis only.
  M  On each frame between 13 and 3 ms before mid-contact (club still in the air, also on the fat shots) where the
     shaft line and ferrule are found: the distance in image rows from the ferrule's centre (sub-pixel) to the
     lowest point of the changed club pixels in a band -2..+6 px toe-ward of the shaft axis (sub-pixel, at
     max(8 DN, 4 sigma)). The same distance on the TS4 model placed on that frame by shaft line + ferrule with the
     61.0 mm length, at the supplied angles and the frame's depth. H = 61.0 + (observed - model) / (image rows per
     mm along the shaft). Per shot: the median of its frames. All four shots, through setup_free.
  RULE, before running: usable in this form only if (1) frames of one swing agree within 2 mm (max - min), (2) the
  four shots' H agree within 4 mm (same club), and (3) with each shot's own H no reading moves away from its mark
  by more than 1 mm against the 61.0 result. All four marks are known now, so this is a stability test, not an
  accuracy test. Script hosel_self.py; results/HOSEL-SELF.txt.
