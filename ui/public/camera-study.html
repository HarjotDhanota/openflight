1. Why these tests

Output resolution is not sensor detail. The lower-resolution OV9281 modes are approximately 2× subsampled; 640×400 mainly adds field of view over 320×200. Native 1280×800 can add spatial samples but is limited to about 120 FPS. We therefore measure spatial evidence, delivered temporal evidence, blur, lighting demand, and final endpoint error separately.

2. Geometry for Cormac's enclosure

Measure the completed design after assembly. Installed orientation is 90° counter-clockwise. The board has seven RF antenna groups (TX1–TX3 and RX1–RX4); each drawn box represents two physical patch elements. Use the four-RX phase-center centroid as the board-frame origin and retain every individual phase center.

board_origin = mean(RX1, RX2, RX3, RX4)
predicted_range_ij(x) = (||x − TX_i|| + ||x − RX_j||) / 2; i=1..3, j=1..4
Measure/storePurpose
Camera optical center translation and roll/pitch/yaw in the RX-centroid board frame.One rigid transform derives camera-relative positions of all seven groups.
TX1–TX3 and RX1–RX4 phase centers from the PCB definition.Preserves all 12 bistatic TX/RX paths and virtual-array geometry.
Lens height, radar height, ball height, target line, tee/net range.Defines metric rays and comparable range.
Mode-specific intrinsics at final focus; multi-position extrinsics with holdout locations.Prevents one-point alignment and mode changes from masquerading as accuracy.
Do not score fusion with placeholder geometry, old-enclosure calibration, or a single averaged antenna point.

On the Pi, stop the OpenFlight kiosk, run bash scripts/start-camera-study.sh once, and open http://127.0.0.1:8765 in the Pi browser. The kiosk and runner must not own the OV9281 together. Configure one arm below, then use the numbered buttons. A clap triggers each calibration view and swing clip.

Required sequence

  1. Run device check and confirm the requested mode appears in rpicam-hello --list-cameras.
  2. Lock focus. For each distinct readout/crop, capture 10–15 board views spanning the frame and solve intrinsics.
  3. At baseline and planned higher light levels, run exposure sweeps, then moving-club clips; a static image cannot validate blur.
  4. Collect five valid 7-iron clips per core arm, alternating matched modes. This is screening, not production proof.
  5. Choose at most two finalists. With frozen processing and fresh geometry, collect driver, wedge, 7-iron, and 9-iron truth data through normal OpenFlight.

Timing starts with free-running frames and the shared sound event. Measure exposure-centred camera contact versus radar range-walk contact and sweep alignment by ±1 delivered frame. External triggering is optional escalation, only if an endpoint exceeds its preregistered error limit; try Pi 5 RP1 hardware PWM before adding a timing board.

5. Measurements and decision rule

AreaRecordGate/use
Mode provenanceRequested/resolved mode, crop, raw format, driver/libcamera, commit.Reject an arm whose actual readout is unknown.
Calibrationfx/fy/cx/cy, distortion, reprojection RMS, edge coverage, file hash.Mode-specific calibration must pass before scoring.
TemporalDelivered FPS, sensor gaps, usable pre/post observations.Zero gaps in every scored clip.
Image evidenceBall diameter, club dimensions, edge uncertainty, smear, contrast, clipped/dark percent.Content remains in frame and moving-target quality passes.
TimingExposure midpoint, camera/radar contact residual, ±1-frame endpoint sensitivity.Uncertainty stays within the preregistered endpoint limit.
GeometrySame-point residual and per-channel residuals for 12 TX/RX pairs.Holdout positions pass; do not tune on truth shots.
TruthMatched reference row, signed/absolute error, availability and withholding reason.Finalist is no worse than baseline with frozen processing.

Decide separately for ball, club delivery, impact detail, and fusion. Compare calibrated spatial uncertainty and delivered observations—not requested pixels/FPS. Reject gains bought with unacceptable blur, noise, clipping, light demand, lost coverage, thermal throttling, or persistence failure. If timing, geometry, or calibration is unmeasured, make no production change.
