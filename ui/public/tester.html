OpenFlight tester

Set up the measured rig, check hardware and light, let the sensors find the ball range, capture the exposure ladder, then review and package the evidence. An accepted capture is saved evidence; it does not establish measurement accuracy.

Before you start

  • Ball at address, unit placed the way you'll hit. The enclosure geometry comes from the checked-in file.
  • Your tester ID is the only typed study field. Before acquisition, you must physically verify and confirm the measured v3 rig setup.
  • For the 1280×800 exposures: foot powder spray and a cloth, for the impact photos.
  • Keep the light the same for the whole session. If you change it, start a new tester ID.

1. Set up the rig

Mandatory setup eligibility

Checking the server-authoritative setup gate…

Required measured v3 placement: shell level; lens centre 95 mm above the floor at the measured adjustable-feet extension; OPS centre 85 mm target-left, 47 mm forward and 20 mm below the lens; IWR RX-row centre 44 mm forward and 30 mm below the lens. OPS and IWR mounts point 10° up. The LIS3DH lies flat with +Y toward the back (180° yaw).

Software can verify the selected config, sensor identity and current tilt evidence. It cannot inspect the enclosure build, feet extension, lens vertex, wiring, or physical orientation.

Deliberate tilted-placement correction tests may cross the 2 degree flag while keeping the sensor mounts, foot extension and optical configuration fixed. The nominal 95 mm lens height is the level-placement baseline, not proof of the lens height after tilting. Record every placement or ball-setup change. The warning is retained; correction accuracy is not validated.

The admission check itself is not an attempt. A setup-gate failure does not count as accepted data or a camera exposure failure. If the operator swung, record it in the independent swing tally.
