OpenFlight tester capture

You collect paired camera and radar recordings. We analyse them and decide settings. This page never shows estimated club or ball results — it shows only whether your capture saved usable data.

Before you start

  • This pilot supports Cormac's enclosure only, so one design is validated first.
  • You need the OV9281 camera, the OPS243, an IWR6843LEVM, and a working sound trigger.
  • Measure your assembled enclosure and enter the numbers below. They ship with your data as provenance.
  • Raw recordings stay out of Git. Send the archive through the agreed channel.
